# Code of Conduct

Solaria follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, welcome people of every background and level of experience, give and take feedback with respect, and keep discussion on the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/jorgehsy). Reports are handled in confidence, and the maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.
